Reproducing the Thinking-Machines Interaction Model

Two models, one mind.

A full-duplex Talker listens, sees and speaks in real time. An asynchronous Thinker reasons alongside it — free to inject a fact or cut in the instant it matters. Bidirectional interruption: you can talk over the AI, and the AI can talk over you.

Talker · MiniCPM-o 4.5 Orchestrator · epoch fence Thinker · async reasoning
01 · TALKER

Real-time, full-duplex

A 9B multimodal model streams speech and text while it keeps listening and watching — input and output never block each other.

02 · ORCHESTRATOR

Epoch fence + floor arbiter

Decides who holds the mic and voids any stale answer or stale cut-in the moment a barge-in resets the epoch.

03 · THINKER

Async deep reasoning

Watches the conversation and emits one of NOOP / INJECT / CUT — quietly correcting or interrupting only when confident.
